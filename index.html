Know your prof

Hitendra Chaturvedi, Professor of Practice. He founded GreenDust (a reverse-logistics startup) and wrote the 5 Rs framework himself. Reverse logistics is his specialty, so don't skip Lesson 6.

His recurring talking points: COVID as "one small pebble in the pond" that created a ripple effect (= bullwhip). On 2025 tariffs he said Walmart, Target and Amazon would feel it first and ship arrivals at LA/Long Beach were down ~35%.

The syllabus says exams test lecture content and are built around the study guide. His slides literally list "Possible Questions". Those are all covered in the lessons.
